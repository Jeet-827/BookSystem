Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2024

redkitindia ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website at redkitindia.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, and payment information when you create an account or make a purchase. We also collect usage data such as pages visited, time spent, and browser type.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process your eBook purchases and deliver download links
  • To send order confirmations and receipts
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Payment Information

Payments are processed securely through Razorpay. We do not store your credit/debit card details on our servers. All transactions are encrypted and comply with PCI-DSS standards.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with trusted service providers (payment processors, email services) solely to operate our platform.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to maintain your session, remember your preferences, and analyze site traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work correctly.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@redkitindia.com for any data-related requests.

8. Contact

For privacy concerns, email us at support@redkitindia.com.
